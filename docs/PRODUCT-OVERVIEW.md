# Nomadly — Product Overview

## Product vision

Nomadly is an India-first social travel and discovery ecosystem that helps people discover destinations, connect with fellow travellers, organize trips and experience local culture.

## Long-term ecosystem modules

1. TravelMate — travel companion discovery.
2. LocalLens — local discovery and hidden places.
3. TravelCircle — traveller communities and meetups.
4. CultureConnect — cultural experiences and local hosts.
5. TripCraft — AI-assisted itinerary planning.
6. WeekendEscape — nearby and short-trip discovery.
7. Footprints — travel journals and memories.
8. TravelBuddy AI — in-trip assistance.
9. EatWithLocals — local food discovery.
10. AdventureHub — outdoor activities and adventure experiences.
11. Festivals & Events Explorer — global festival discovery, calendars, schedules, attire guidance and cultural etiquette.

## MVP focus

The first release validates three behaviours:

- People discover destinations.
- People connect with fellow travellers.
- People organize trips together.

The starter repository includes initial UI routes for Explore, Connect, Trips, Festivals and Profile. These are not all backed by production workflows yet.
