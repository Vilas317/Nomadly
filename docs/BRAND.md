# Nomadly Brand & Experience Direction

**Working name:** Nomadly  
**Product line:** Discover. Connect. Plan. Book. Experience.  
**Positioning:** A social travel and discovery ecosystem, beginning in India and designed for global expansion.

## Experience principle

The product should feel calm, premium, human and quietly inspiring from the first screen. It should invite exploration rather than overwhelm users with booking widgets, dense dashboards or loud promotional UI.

The visual language takes inspiration from the restraint and polish of Apple's official digital experiences without copying Apple's layouts, assets, marks or proprietary design. Travel-specific warmth, editorial photography and cultural context make the interface distinctly Nomadly.

## Design pillars

1. **Calm by default** — generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, limited simultaneous calls to action and restrained use of colour.
2. **A sense of place** — large, high-quality destination imagery, meaningful captions and editorial storytelling.
3. **Human connection** — approachable profiles, respectful language, clear safety cues and warm community surfaces.
4. **Confident simplicity** — familiar navigation, predictable interactions, readable typography and progressive disclosure.
5. **Trust before conversion** — distinguish verified facts from community recommendations, sponsored content and illustrative demo data.
6. **Responsive by design** — desktop should feel spacious; mobile should feel purpose-built, with thumb-friendly controls and compact navigation.

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** soft white `#FCFCFA`, not stark blue-white.
- **Primary ink:** deep charcoal-green `#18231F`.
- **Forest:** `#203F32` for primary actions and anchor elements.
- **Soft sage:** `#E5EEE7` for supportive surfaces.
- **Warm sand:** `#F4F0E8` for occasional section contrast.
- **Accent:** muted terracotta `#C77B52`, used sparingly.
- **Typography:** neutral modern sans-serif for interface and most headings; expressive editorial type may be introduced selectively for destination storytelling. Avoid mixing too many font families.
- **Photography:** authentic, immersive, location-specific, high-resolution imagery. Avoid generic corporate stock photography and repeated image treatments.
- **Shape:** softly rounded cards and pills; avoid excessive nesting of cards.
- **Motion:** subtle hover lift, gentle image transitions and short feedback animations. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Layout guidance

- Homepage: one confident cinematic hero, then curated destinations, human connection, festivals/events and the wider travel ecosystem.
- Explore: image-led discovery with useful filters and concise location metadata.
- Connect: profile-first, safety-aware, non-gamified traveller discovery.
- Trips: calm planning workspace with a clear itinerary hierarchy.
- Festivals: editorial event cards with date confidence and source verification.
- Booking gateway: contextual options, clear external-provider labels and transparent sponsored/affiliate disclosures. Do not make the experience feel like an ad marketplace.

## Content voice

Warm, clear, curious and respectful. Prefer specific language over generic hype. Do not promise safety, live availability, verified information or AI accuracy unless the underlying feature and evidence support the claim.

## Accessibility and quality bar

- Maintain WCAG AA colour contrast for body text and controls.
- Keep keyboard focus visible.
- Use semantic headings and labelled inputs.
- Provide alt text for meaningful images.
- Ensure touch targets are comfortably sized on mobile.
- Avoid relying on colour alone to communicate status.
- Test at narrow mobile, tablet and wide desktop breakpoints.

## Implementation note

The current starter applies the initial visual direction through shared CSS and existing page components. It is a foundation, not a final art-directed product. Before public launch, replace remote demo imagery with licensed/owned optimized assets, validate accessibility, and test the UI with real users.
